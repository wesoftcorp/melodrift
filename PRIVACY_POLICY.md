# Melodrift Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 2, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 2, 2026  

Melodrift ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Melodrift handles, how it is collected and used, and how you can manage or delete your data when using the Melodrift application on Android, Windows, and web platforms.

Melodrift is an independent audio player and media streaming application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and Handle

Melodrift is designed with a privacy-first approach, keeping the vast majority of your data locally on your device.

### A. Local Device Data
Melodrift stores certain information locally on your device to ensure smooth playback and offline functionality:
* **App Preferences:** Audio quality settings, 10-band equalizer presets, theme selection (dark mode / glassmorphism), and language preferences.
* **Local Library:** Playlists, playback queue, and listening history created locally.
* **Offline Music Cache:** Audio tracks, album covers, and lyric files cached or downloaded for offline playback.
* **Local Logs:** Diagnostic log files generated strictly for local app troubleshooting.

### B. Cloud Sync & Account Data (Optional)
If you choose to use cloud backup and cross-device sync features:
* **Authentication:** When signing in, Melodrift receives basic identity information (email address, display name, and profile picture) to authenticate your account and partition your cloud library.
* **Cloud Playlists & Favorites:** Your saved playlists, liked tracks, and synced library records are stored securely in your private cloud partition.
* **No Password Storage:** Melodrift does not collect or store passwords; all authentication is handled securely through industry-standard OAuth authentication.

### C. Search Queries and Metadata
* When you search for music, explore discovery categories, stream tracks, or view album artwork, Melodrift queries public online audio indexes and metadata providers. These requests transmit the search terms or content identifiers necessary to fetch song titles, artist names, album art, lyrics, and audio streams.

---

## 2. Device Permissions and Why We Need Them

Melodrift requests only the minimum permissions necessary to deliver core audio playback and user-initiated features:

* **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`):**
  * *Purpose:* Required only if you use the optional **Voice Search** feature.
  * *Data Handling:* Audio input is processed ephemerally on your device solely to transcribe spoken search queries into text. Melodrift **never** records audio in the background, never stores your voice recordings, and never sends audio data to remote servers for advertising or profiling.
* **Foreground Service & Media Playback (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`):**
  * *Purpose:* Enables continuous background music playback and provides interactive media playback controls (play, pause, skip, seek) in the notification bar and lock screen.
* **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):**
  * *Purpose:* Displays the media playback notification with album art and playback controls on Android 13 and above.
* **Storage & Audio Media (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` / Storage Access):**
  * *Purpose:* Allows the app to index and play local audio files stored on your device and save tracks for offline listening within the app. Melodrift does not access personal files, photos, or documents outside its intended media scope.

---

## 3. Advertising and Tracking

* **100% Ad-Free:** Melodrift does not display ads.
* **No Advertising Identifiers:** Melodrift does not collect or use the Google Advertising ID (`AD_ID`) or other advertising tracking identifiers.
* **No Data Selling:** We do **not** sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, listening habits, or library information to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

---

## 4. Diagnostics and Performance

To maintain app stability and resolve crashes, Melodrift may collect anonymous, non-identifying technical diagnostic reports:
* **Crash Reports & Performance:** Basic device information (device model, operating system version, app version, and stack traces) to diagnose application crashes and performance bottlenecks.
* These diagnostic logs do not contain personal information or listening history.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

### Local Data Deletion
* You can delete downloaded songs, clear search history, or clear your local cache at any time directly within the app settings.
* Uninstalling the application automatically removes all local app data and cached files from your device.

### Cloud Data & Account Deletion
In accordance with Google Play Store policies, you have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and associated cloud data:
* **In-App:** Go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account / Clear Cloud Data**.
* **Web Request:** You can request permanent deletion of your cloud records and account data at any time by emailing **`rajeev.upadhyay@live.in`** or visiting our official website at [https://melodrift.rajeevupadhyay.com](https://melodrift.rajeevupadhyay.com). Upon request, all associated cloud library data, playlists, and user identifiers will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

## 6. Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete such data.
