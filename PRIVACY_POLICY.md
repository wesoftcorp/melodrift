# Melodrift Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 25, 2026

Melodrift is a music discovery and playback app for Windows and Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information Melodrift handles, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Melodrift is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube, YouTube Music, Google, Microsoft, or any other music service provider.

## Information Melodrift Handles

Melodrift is designed to keep most app data on your device. Depending on which features you use, the app may handle the following information.

### Local App Data

Melodrift may store data locally on your device, including:

- App settings and preferences.
- Theme and playback preferences.
- Local playlists and queue state.
- Recently loaded discovery, search, or home-feed cache data.
- Download records and app-only offline playback files.
- Local logs needed for troubleshooting app behavior.

This data is used to make the app work, improve startup speed, remember your preferences, and support local/offline playback features.

### Music Search, Streaming, and Metadata

When you search for music, browse discovery content, stream songs, or load artwork, Melodrift may send network requests to online music or metadata sources. These requests may include search terms, content identifiers, app/network request information, and technical metadata needed to retrieve playable streams, artwork, titles, artists, albums, playlists, or related recommendations.

Those online services may process requests according to their own privacy policies and terms. Melodrift does not control how third-party services process data once requests are sent to them.

### Offline Downloads

If you use offline playback features, Melodrift may save app-managed music files on your device. These files are intended for playback inside Melodrift only. The app may store file paths, song identifiers, integrity information, and encryption-related metadata needed to manage downloads and playback.

Deleting a download in Melodrift removes the app-managed local copy. Uninstall behavior may depend on the operating system and how the app package stores local data.

### Microphone and Voice Features

If you use voice search or song recognition features, Melodrift may request microphone access. Microphone input is used only to perform the feature you choose, such as recognizing speech or analyzing nearby audio for song recognition.

Melodrift does not continuously record microphone audio in the background for advertising or profiling. Microphone access can be controlled through your operating system settings.

### Optional Account, Cloud, or Collaboration Features

Some builds or future versions of Melodrift may include optional account, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, or collaboration features. If enabled, those features may use third-party services such as Firebase or Google services.

These features may process account identifiers, authentication status, collaboration room data, diagnostics, crash reports, or analytics events needed to provide the feature. If a feature is optional, you can choose not to use it.

The current Windows Store package does not require sign-in for basic playback, search, local playlists, or app-only offline downloads.

## How Information Is Used

Melodrift uses information to:

- Provide music discovery, search, playback, playlists, queue controls, and offline playback.
- Save app preferences and local library data.
- Improve app performance through caching.
- Maintain download integrity and app-only playback behavior.
- Troubleshoot crashes, errors, or playback problems when diagnostics are available.
- Support optional features that you choose to use.

Melodrift does not sell your personal information.

## Data Sharing

Melodrift may share limited information only when needed for app functionality, such as:

- Sending search, streaming, metadata, artwork, or recognition requests to relevant online services.
- Sending diagnostics or crash information to configured diagnostic providers, if enabled in the build or by user settings.
- Complying with legal obligations, security requirements, or valid requests from authorities where applicable.

Melodrift does not sell personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

## Third-Party Services

Melodrift may interact with third-party services for music discovery, streaming, metadata, artwork, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, or cloud features. Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Examples of third-party service categories may include:

- Music and media metadata providers.
- Streaming or content platforms.
- Authentication providers.
- Analytics and crash reporting providers.
- Cloud database or sync providers.

You should review the privacy policies of any third-party services used with the app.

## Data Retention

Local app data is kept on your device until you delete it, clear app data, remove downloads, reset settings, or uninstall the app.

Cached data may be refreshed or replaced automatically to keep app content current. Downloaded app-only files remain until deleted by you, cleared by the app, or removed by the operating system during uninstall or data cleanup.

Data handled by third-party services is retained according to those services' policies.

## Your Choices

You can control your information by:

- Clearing app cache or local data where the app provides controls.
- Deleting downloaded songs inside Melodrift.
- Removing local playlists or queue items.
- Disabling microphone permission in Windows or Android settings.
- Choosing not to use optional account, cloud, collaboration, or diagnostic features.
- Uninstalling the app to remove app-managed local data, subject to operating system behavior.

## Children's Privacy

Melodrift is not directed to children under 13. The app can access online music content that may include explicit lyrics or mature themes depending on the source content. Parents or guardians should supervise use by minors and use operating system or store-level family controls where appropriate.

## Security

Melodrift uses reasonable technical measures to protect app-managed local data, including app-only storage and encrypted local download files where supported. No app, network service, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## International Users

Online services used by Melodrift may process information in different countries or regions. By using features that connect to online services, you understand that data may be processed outside your country or region according to the policies of those services.

## Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If material changes are made, the effective date will be updated. Continued use of Melodrift after changes means you accept the updated policy.
