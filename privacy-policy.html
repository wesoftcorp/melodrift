Melodrift Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 25, 2026

Melodrift is a music discovery and playback app for Windows and Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information Melodrift handles, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Melodrift is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube, YouTube Music, Google, Microsoft, or any other music service provider.

Information Melodrift Handles

Melodrift is designed to keep most app data on your device. Depending on which features you use, the app may handle local app data, music search and streaming metadata, offline download records, microphone input for voice features, and optional account or diagnostic data if those features are enabled.

Local App Data

Melodrift may store app settings, theme and playback preferences, local playlists, queue state, cached discovery data, download records, app-only offline playback files, and local troubleshooting logs on your device. This data is used to make the app work, improve startup speed, remember your preferences, and support offline playback.

Music Search, Streaming, and Metadata

When you search for music, browse discovery content, stream songs, or load artwork, Melodrift may send network requests to online music or metadata sources. These requests may include search terms, content identifiers, app or network request information, and technical metadata needed to retrieve streams, artwork, titles, artists, albums, playlists, or recommendations.

Those online services may process requests according to their own privacy policies and terms. Melodrift does not control how third-party services process data once requests are sent to them.

Offline Downloads

If you use offline playback features, Melodrift may save app-managed music files on your device. These files are intended for playback inside Melodrift only. The app may store file paths, song identifiers, integrity information, and encryption-related metadata needed to manage downloads and playback.

Microphone and Voice Features

If you use voice search or song recognition features, Melodrift may request microphone access. Microphone input is used only to perform the feature you choose, such as recognizing speech or analyzing nearby audio for song recognition. Melodrift does not continuously record microphone audio in the background for advertising or profiling.

Optional Account, Cloud, or Collaboration Features

Some builds or future versions of Melodrift may include optional account, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, or collaboration features. If enabled, those features may use third-party services such as Firebase or Google services. The current Windows Store package does not require sign-in for basic playback, search, local playlists, or app-only offline downloads.

How Information Is Used

Melodrift uses information to provide music discovery, search, playback, playlists, queue controls, offline playback, app preferences, caching, download integrity, troubleshooting, and optional features that you choose to use. Melodrift does not sell your personal information.

Data Sharing

Melodrift may share limited information only when needed for app functionality, such as sending search, streaming, metadata, artwork, or recognition requests to relevant online services; sending diagnostics or crash information if enabled; or complying with legal obligations. Melodrift does not sell personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

Third-Party Services

Melodrift may interact with third-party services for music discovery, streaming, metadata, artwork, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, or cloud features. Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms, and you should review them when using related features.

Data Retention

Local app data is kept on your device until you delete it, clear app data, remove downloads, reset settings, or uninstall the app. Cached data may be refreshed or replaced automatically. Data handled by third-party services is retained according to those services' policies.

Your Choices

You can clear app cache or local data where controls are provided, delete downloaded songs, remove local playlists or queue items, disable microphone permission in Windows or Android settings, avoid optional online features, or uninstall the app to remove app-managed local data subject to operating system behavior.

Children's Privacy

Melodrift is not directed to children under 13. The app can access online music content that may include explicit lyrics or mature themes depending on the source content. Parents or guardians should supervise use by minors and use operating system or store-level family controls where appropriate.

Security

Melodrift uses reasonable technical measures to protect app-managed local data, including app-only storage and encrypted local download files where supported. No app, network service, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If material changes are made, the effective date will be updated. Continued use of Melodrift after changes means you accept the updated policy.
